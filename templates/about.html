Why KisanSetu Exists

Fixing the Agricultural Supply Chain

The Traditional Problem

Multiple Intermediaries

Farmer → Local Aggregator → Mandi Broker → Wholesaler → Retailer → Customer

  • Farmers receive a smaller share of the final selling price.
  • Customers pay inflated retail prices for produce that has been stored and handled for days.
  • Growers lack visibility into upcoming customer demand, leading to overproduction or shortages.
The KisanSetu Solution

Direct Farm-to-Home Bridge

Farmer → KisanSetu → Customer

  • Farmers list produce directly and set their own fair price per kg.
  • Customers browse transparent prices with optional market price comparisons.
  • Built-in 7-day moving average demand forecasting helps farmers plan inventory and harvests.

Core Platform Capabilities

Transparent Pricing

Compare direct farmer prices against optional reference market prices with zero hidden markups.

Demand Forecasting

7-day moving average analytics computed from real non-rejected orders to estimate weekly demand.

Cash on Delivery

Simple, accessible checkout with automatic stock tracking and single-farmer order fulfillment.

Role-Based Security

Dedicated Farmer Hub, Customer Dashboard, and Admin Control Center with strict ownership checks.

How KisanSetu Works

Farmer → KisanSetu → Customer

List 01
Farmers List Products

Farmers register and upload their fresh vegetables, fruits, grains, and pulses with fair prices and available stock.

Order 02
Customers Browse & Buy

Customers search the public marketplace, compare prices, add fresh produce to cart, and place Cash on Delivery orders.

Deliver 03
Direct Fulfillment & Insights

Farmers accept and fulfill orders directly while using 7-day demand forecasting to plan upcoming harvests.
